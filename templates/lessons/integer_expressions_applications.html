2-minute recap

Order of operations and signed changes

Grouping first, then multiply and divide from left to right, then add and subtract from left to right. In stories, a rise or gain is positive and a fall or loss is negative.

Expressions: the order of operations

  1. Grouping first: ( ), [ ], { }.
  2. Multiply and divide, from left to right.
  3. Add and subtract, from left to right.

Worked example

Evaluate 4 + 2 x (-3).

  1. Multiply before you add: 2 x (-3) = -6.
  2. Then add: 4 + (-6) = -2.

-2

Common mistake

6 + 8 / (-2) = -7 is wrong. That adds before dividing. Divide first: 8 / (-2) = -4, then 6 + (-4) = 2.

Signed quantities: changes in real life

Words that mean signs

Rise, gain, deposit and up mean positive changes. Fall, loss, withdrawal and down mean negative changes. Add the change to where you start.

Interpreting the result

Always include the unit. Say whether the result is positive or negative, and what it means.

Worked example

A diver is at -12 m and rises 5 m. Where is the diver now?

  1. The start is -12. "Rises" is a positive change: +5.
  2. Add: -12 + 5 = -7.
  3. Interpret: the diver is 7 m below sea level.

-7 m (7 m below sea level)

Common mistake

Writing a fall of 6 degrees as +6 is wrong. A fall is a negative change, so write -6.
