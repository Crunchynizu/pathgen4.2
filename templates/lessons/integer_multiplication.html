2-minute recap

Multiplying integers

Multiply the distances first, then decide the sign. Same signs give a positive product; different signs give a negative product.

Worked example

Compute (-6) x 4.

  1. The signs are different, so the product is negative.
  2. Multiply the distances: 6 x 4 = 24.

(-6) x 4 = -24

Multiplication

Work with the distances first. Then decide the sign.

  • (-6) x 4 = -24
  • 6 x (-4) = -24
  • (-6) x (-4) = 24

More than two factors

Count the negative factors. An even number of negatives gives a positive product; an odd number gives a negative product. Example: (-1) x (-2) x (-3) = -6.

Common mistake

(-7) x 3 = 21 is wrong. The signs are different, so the answer is -21.
