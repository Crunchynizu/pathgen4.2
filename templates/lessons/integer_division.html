2-minute recap

Dividing integers

Division uses the same sign rules as multiplication. Divide the distances first, then decide the sign.

Worked example

Compute (-24) / (-6).

  1. The signs are the same, so the quotient is positive.
  2. Divide the distances: 24 / 6 = 4.

(-24) / (-6) = 4

Division

Same signs give a positive result. Different signs give a negative result.

  • (-24) / (-6) = 4
  • 24 / (-6) = -4
  • (-24) / 6 = -4

Check with multiplication

Division undoes multiplication. To check 24 / (-6) = -4, multiply back: (-4) x (-6) = 24.

Common mistake

(-24) / 6 = 4 is wrong. The signs are different, so the answer is -4.

Division builds on multiplication.
