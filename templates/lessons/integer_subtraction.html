2-minute recap

Subtracting integers

Subtracting an integer is the same as adding its opposite: a - b = a + (-b).

Try it

Rewrite 5 - (-2) as an addition

  1. Keep the first number: 5.
  2. Change the subtraction to addition: 5 + ...
  3. Change -2 to its opposite, 2: 5 + 2.

5 - (-2) = 5 + 2 = 7

Worked example

Compute -3 - 6.

  1. Add the opposite of 6: -3 + (-6).
  2. Same signs: add the distances, 3 + 6 = 9, and keep the negative sign.

-3 - 6 = -9

Subtraction

Subtracting an integer is the same as adding its opposite. a - b = a + (-b).

  • 4 - (-3) = 4 + 3 = 7
  • 2 - 6 = 2 + (-6) = -4
  • -8 - (-5) = -8 + 5 = -3

On a number line, subtracting a positive number moves left, and subtracting a negative number moves right.

Common mistake

4 - (-3) = 1 is wrong. Subtracting -3 means adding 3, so 4 - (-3) = 7.

Subtraction builds on opposites and addition.
